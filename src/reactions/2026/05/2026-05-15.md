From a [tweet](https://x.com/matteocollina/status/1843730801143275850) by Matteo Collina

> Got a few things to rectify: 
> 1. Hono is missing quite a few things that come out of the box in Fastify. You might not need them to start, but they are there when you need them (like validations, za proper logger, a plugin system, or lifecycle hooks). 
> 2. Hono is not fast (at least on Node.js), and their benchmark claims are fundamentally flawed, with major performance cliffs when things get complex. 
> 3. Hono monkeypatches Node.js, so it’s more likely to break as releases come out. 
> The real benefit is that Hono is multi-runtime. It’s sponsored by Cloudflare and it’s great there.
